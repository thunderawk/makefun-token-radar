How the value is worked out

Every figure comes from the sealed snapshot (DefiLlama, ). No name is ever special-cased.

  1. Multiple. The protocol's category multiple from the Calibration tab.
  2. Base. TVL × multiple.
  3. Growth. 1 + clamp(7-day TVL change, −60%, +120%) / 100 × 0.5.
  4. Maturity. 0.6 + min(age in days, 1095) / 1095 × 0.4. Unknown age counts as 365 days.
  5. Breadth. 1 + 0.02 per extra chain, at most 1.10.
  6. Implied value. Base × growth × maturity × breadth, rounded to whole dollars.

What "pre-token" means

A protocol records no token when DefiLlama carries no market cap, no CoinGecko id and no symbol for it. That is a record, not proof — a listed protocol may still have a token its record does not carry. Wrapped, staked and bridged products of tokens that already trade are kept off the board.
